UAEU Abu Saif Platform

A group of UAE University students have created Bu Saif – a robot that speaks Arabic and teaches UAE history and culture. Dressed in traditional Emirati attire, a kandura and ghutra, Bu Saif is being displayed at the annual Gitex Technology Week at the Dubai World Trade Centre until October 10. The students are keen on getting an actual Emirati passport for Bu Saif, who has been designed for working at a museum to teach visitors about all things UAE.
